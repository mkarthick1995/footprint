# Competition & prior art (researched 2026-10-07 → 10-10)

| Product | What it does | Gap we exploit |
|---|---|---|
| Gemini Live "Guided Vision" (Google) | Voice visual assistant for blind/low-vision on Android; reported launch ~2026-10-01. Disclaims navigation & obstacle detection | We do navigation, hazard alerts, and a shared map — what Google explicitly left out |
| Be My Eyes (+ Meta glasses) | Volunteer video calls + AI descriptions | Human-dependent; no proactive alerts or mapping |
| Oorion, Scribe Me (Meta glasses) | Object finding, obstacle detection (demo-stage) | No community map; not tuned for JAPAC streets |
| Envision, Seeing AI, Aira | Reading, scene description, paid human agents | Description-centric; no crowd-sourced accessibility data |

**Our one-line differentiation:** personal assistance that turns every walk into community accessibility infrastructure,
built for JAPAC street conditions.

Judges from Google Cloud will likely know Guided Vision — address it head-on in the deck.
